🚨 Safety reminders ▼
Side gates — latch every time you pass through
Whenever you walk between the backyard and the front of the house. Both dogs will bolt into the neighborhood the moment a gate is left unlatched, even for a second. If Henny gets out: call his name and run the opposite direction — he'll follow.
Henny's crate on the patio
It's his chill zone and safe space. Lyra must NEVER enter. Do not put any toys or objects in or near it — if a toy rolls in and Lyra goes for it, Henny will attack her badly. Keep it clear.
Dog fight (very rare)
If Henny won't let go: apply pressure on his backside — the shock releases his grip. Or use a chair to separate. Your safety first, then Lyra, then Henny.
Toilet seats — always down
Tuxy will go in and run wet-pawed everywhere in the house.
Linguini stays indoors
Not street savvy. Guest room and kitchen only — not the two other bedrooms.
If a chicken is lost to a hawk
Sad but it happens — we've lost a few. Don't panic, don't feel bad. Animal control: bag carcass in regular trash. No pickup. Just text us.
